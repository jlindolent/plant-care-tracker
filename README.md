# 🌱 Plant Care Tracker

A personalized plant care management system that helps you keep your plants thriving by tracking individual care schedules and delivering timely reminders.

## The Problem

Plant owners don't kill their plants from lack of care — they kill them from inconsistent care. A succulent needs water every 2-3 weeks, a fern twice weekly, and a snake plant thrives on neglect. Without a system to track these varying needs, plant owners default to guesswork.

## The Solution

Plant Care Tracker treats each plant as an individual with its own care schedule. Add your plants, customize their care routines, and receive timely reminders when each plant needs attention.

## Key Features

- **Plant Inventory Management** — Track all your plants with species-specific information
- **Customizable Care Schedules** — Set watering, fertilizing, and pruning schedules per plant
- **Smart Reminders** — Get notifications when each plant needs care, not generic alerts
- **Care History** — Log and review what you've done to learn what works

## What Makes This Different

- **Adaptive, not prescriptive** — Starts with species defaults but adapts to your environment
- **Focused simplicity** — Does one thing exceptionally well without feature bloat
- **Accessible** — Core features available without aggressive paywalls
